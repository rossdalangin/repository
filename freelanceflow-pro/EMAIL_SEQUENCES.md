# FreelanceFlow Pro: High-Converting Email Sequences

This document contains automated email sequences for onboarding and tier upgrades.

---

## 📧 Sequence 1: 7-Day "Welcome to Pro" (Onboarding)

### Day 1: The Fast-Start
**Subject:** ⚡ You're Pro now. Let's generate your first contract.
"Welcome to the inner circle. Most freelancers spend hours on contracts; you're about to do it in 60 seconds. Head to the **Template Generator** and click 'Independent Contractor Agreement'. Use the 'Load Sample' button to see how the pros do it."

### Day 3: Organization is Power
**Subject:** 🔒 Did you see your new secure File Vault?
"Security is a major selling point for high-ticket clients. Upload your client IDs and signed agreements to your private vault. You can now send secure, expiring links instead of messy email attachments."

### Day 5: The "Lost Draft" Problem
**Subject:** 📂 Never lose a document again.
"Did you know every PDF and DOCX you generate is automatically archived in your **'My Generated Docs'** folder? It's your personal business library, available 24/7."

### Day 7: The Scaling Question
**Subject:** 📈 Ready to build a team?
"You're crushing your solo work. But what happens when you have more leads than you can handle? That's when you move to the **Agency Tier**. I'll show you how to monetize a team in my next email."
